# Hi!👋
### 🚀 DevOps & SRE Engineer | Passionate about Automation, Reliability & Scalability
I am a DevOps and SRE Engineer with about 3 years of professional experience. My career journey started deep in application code and evolved into managing complex infrastructures. This diverse background allows me not only to master infrastructure but also to deeply understand the needs of development teams and resolve system bottlenecks with a holistic view.
---
### 🛤️ My Career Journey (From Dev to Infra)
Hands-on experience across all stages of the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) has shaped my perspective on system architecture and reliability:
- 💻 **Software Development & QA:**
My first steps in the software world were as an **Angular** developer. Later, to ensure high-quality outputs, I took on Quality Assurance (QA) responsibilities and implemented comprehensive UI, Performance, and API testing using tools like `Selenium`, `K6`, `Playwright`, and `JavaScript`.
- ⚙️ **Backend & Business Logic:**
I spent about 2 years highly focused on process development using **BPMN** and **C#**. During this time, I gained valuable experience in backend architecture and working with robust databases such as `SQL Server`, `Oracle`, and `PostgreSQL`.
- ☁️ **Entering the DevOps & SRE World:**
Ultimately, my strong passion for service stability and automation drew me into the heart of infrastructure and the DevOps ecosystem. Currently, my main focus is on designing scalable clusters, setting up integrated **CI/CD** pipelines, and implementing **Observability** systems.
