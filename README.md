# Hi there! 👋

### 🚀 DevOps & SRE Engineer | Kubernetes, GitOps, Monitoring & Observability, and AI Infrastructure (MLOps)

I am a **DevOps and Site Reliability Engineer (SRE)** with over **4 years of professional experience** across the full Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), working with enterprise systems and large-scale projects.

My professional journey started in Frontend Development and evolved through Quality Assurance (QA), Performance Engineering, and Systems Engineering before I eventually specialized in Cloud-Native Infrastructure.

This comprehensive experience across all layers of software production has given me a holistic understanding of software architecture. It enables me to effectively bridge the gap between development and operations teams, identify deep system bottlenecks, optimize performance under heavy workloads, and ensure High Availability (HA).

---

### 🛤️ My Career Journey (From Development & QA to Infrastructure & AI)

Hands-on experience across every layer of software production has shaped my professional approach to system reliability, stability, and scalability.

- 💻 **Frontend Development & QA/Performance Engineering:**

  I began my professional career developing enterprise web applications using Angular and technologies such as `PrimeNG`, `HTML5/SASS`, and `RESTful APIs`.

  I then transitioned into QA Automation and Performance Engineering, working as a Senior Engineer in this field.

  During this phase, I designed and implemented comprehensive frameworks for UI, API, Load, and Stress Testing based on `ISTQB` standards, using tools such as `Playwright`, `Cypress`, `Selenium`, `k6`, and `Apache JMeter`.

  This experience built a strong foundation for analyzing system behavior under high-pressure conditions and heavy workloads.

- ⚙️ **Systems Engineering, BPMN & Data Architecture:**

  During my time as a Systems Engineer, I focused on designing and modeling organizational workflows using the `BPMN` standard, performing data analysis, and integrating backend systems using `C#` and enterprise databases, including `SQL Server`, `PostgreSQL`, and `Oracle`.

  This phase strengthened my understanding of complex business logic and large-scale enterprise system integration.

- ☁️ **DevOps, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) & Cloud-Native Infrastructure:**

  Driven by my passion for automation and service reliability, I fully transitioned into DevOps and SRE, focusing on mission-critical and high-traffic platforms.

  My primary areas of expertise include:

  - **Container Orchestration & GitOps:** Migrating, deploying, and managing services and workloads on production `Kubernetes` clusters using tools such as `Rancher` and `Helm`; implementing automated CI/CD pipelines with `GitLab CI`; managing GitOps workflows with `ArgoCD`; and managing container image and software artifact registries using `Harbor` and `Nexus`.
  - **Full-Stack Observability:** Designing and implementing comprehensive architectures for monitoring, log collection and management, and distributed tracing using tools such as `Prometheus`, `Grafana`, `ELK Stack`, `OpenTelemetry`, and `Jaeger`.
  - **AI & Large Language Model Infrastructure (MLOps / LLMOps):** Deploying, scaling, and troubleshooting inference infrastructure for AI models and Large Language Models (LLMs), as well as managing AI processing pipelines involving `Embedder`, `Reranker`, `OCR`, and `NeMo-STT`.
  - **Site Reliability & Incident Management:** Ensuring High Availability (HA), designing and implementing Disaster Recovery (DR) solutions, and responding rapidly to incidents and service disruptions across large-scale and complex infrastructures.
